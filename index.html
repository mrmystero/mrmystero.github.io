THE BACKROOMS

You have noclipped out of reality in incorrect areas. There is no going back, but there might be a way out.

What Is It?

This is a suspenseful 3D survival game built entirely using web native technologies. Trapped inside Level 0—the classic "Lobby" of the Backrooms—you are surrounded by randomly segmented rooms, mono-yellow wallpaper, and the maddening hum of fluorescent lights.

Your sole objective is to track down and harness 10 glowing power cubes hidden within the environment. Feeding these cubes into the heavy facility security locks is your only ticket back to reality.

The Inspiration

Inspired by the legendary internet creepypastas and liminal space aesthetics, this project handles the horrors of isolation with a creative, modern twist.

It blends the vintage, analog dread of early internet urban legends with reactive mechanics. Every pillar and corridor is procedurally textured to deliver that heavy, crushing atmosphere of a place you shouldn't be, mixed with arcade-style pacing.

Survival Guide

The Mechanics

Time is running out. You have exactly 7 minutes before the exit system locks completely, trapping you inside the hum forever.

W A S D Navigate the halls SHIFT Sprint (Drains Stamina) MOUSE Look around (and Flashlight) E Interact / Escape at the Door

The Stalker

You are not completely alone in the damp halls. A manifestation of the environment drifts endlessly through the corridors searching for intruders.

⚠️ THREAT ASSESSMENT

The entity hunts predominantly by sight. It will accelerate drastically upon catching your line of sight. If spotted, immediately duck behind heavy pillars or break line of sight to force it back into its patrol cycle.

Ensure your audio is turned on. Headphones are recommended.
